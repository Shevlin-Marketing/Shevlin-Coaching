Privacy Policy.

How we collect, use and protect your personal data.

Last updated: May 2025

1. Who We Are

Shevlin Coaching is operated by Ayden Shevlin. We are the data controller for personal data collected through our website and programmes. You can contact us at sales@shevlinmarketing.org.

2. What Data We Collect

2.1 Application Form Data

When you submit an application through our website, we collect:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Location
  • Current employment and income information
  • Your responses to programme-related questions

2.2 Payment Data

Payment is processed by Stripe. We do not store your card details. Stripe's privacy policy is available at stripe.com/gb/privacy.

2.3 Programme Participation Data

During the programme, we may collect notes from coaching sessions, call recordings (where consent has been given), and progress information.

2.4 Communications Data

If you contact us by email or WhatsApp, we retain records of that communication.

2.5 Website Data

Our website may collect standard technical data including your IP address, browser type, and pages visited. This is used only for security and basic analytics purposes.

3. How We Use Your Data

We use your personal data to:

  • Process and assess your programme application
  • Deliver the programme you have enrolled on
  • Communicate with you about your programme, calls, and community
  • Process your payment
  • Improve our programmes and services
  • Comply with legal obligations

We do not use your data for automated decision-making or profiling, and we do not sell your data to third parties.

4. Legal Basis for Processing

We process your data under the following legal bases:

  • Contract — processing necessary to deliver the programme you have paid for
  • Legitimate interests — improving our services and communicating with applicants
  • Legal obligation — where required by law
  • Consent — where you have given specific consent, such as for call recordings

5. Who We Share Your Data With

We share your data only with:

  • Stripe — for payment processing
  • Formspree — for application form submission processing
  • Google — for Google Drive file storage and Google Sheets used for applicant tracking
  • Zoom — for live call delivery

All third-party providers are required to handle your data securely and in accordance with applicable data protection law. We do not share your data with any other third parties without your explicit consent.

6. How Long We Keep Your Data

  • Application data — retained for 12 months from the date of application, then securely deleted
  • Programme participant data — retained for 3 years from the end of your programme, then securely deleted
  • Payment records — retained for 7 years as required for financial record-keeping
  • Call recordings — retained for 6 months from the date of recording, then deleted

7. Your Rights

Under UK GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Access — request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Rectification — request that inaccurate data is corrected
  • Erasure — request that your data is deleted, subject to our legal obligations
  • Restriction — request that we restrict processing of your data in certain circumstances
  • Portability — receive your data in a commonly used format
  • Objection — object to processing based on legitimate interests
  • Withdraw consent — where processing is based on consent, withdraw it at any time

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at sales@shevlinmarketing.org. We will respond within 30 days.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your personal data from unauthorised access, loss, or misuse. All data is stored on password-protected systems and shared only with the third-party providers listed above.

9. Cookies

Our website may use basic cookies for functionality purposes. We do not currently use tracking or advertising cookies. If this changes, this policy will be updated and you will be notified.

10. Complaints

If you have concerns about how we handle your data, please contact us first at sales@shevlinmarketing.org. If you remain unsatisfied, you have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. Continued use of our services following changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

12. Contact

For any questions about this privacy policy or how we handle your data, contact us at sales@shevlinmarketing.org.
